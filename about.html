About PPFees

PPFees.com is an independent collection of free payment-fee and seller-planning tools created by James Gore. Its purpose is to help individuals and small businesses understand the practical difference between the amount a customer pays and the amount a seller keeps.

Rather than showing a single fee figure without context, we explain how processing methods, fixed charges, international payments, transaction size, and ordinary business costs affect the result.

How we approach accuracy

We refer to the published US PayPal merchant fee schedule, disclose the date and scope of the reference, provide worked examples, and make the formulas visible. Actual merchant statements and PayPal's current terms take precedence over our estimates.

Independent resource

PPFees is not owned, sponsored, or endorsed by PayPal. PayPal is a trademark of its respective owner. Our website provides educational calculations rather than payment processing, financial advice, or account access.
