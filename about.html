About PPFees

PPFees.com is a free, independent educational calculator designed to help individuals and small businesses estimate transaction costs before requesting or receiving a payment.

Our goal is to make percentage-based and fixed payment fees understandable, with clear calculations, editable inputs and straightforward explanations.
