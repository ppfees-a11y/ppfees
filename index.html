Understand your PayPal fees before sending an invoice

Payment processing costs can affect your profit on freelance projects, digital products, services, and online sales. The most useful calculation is often not just the fee itself, but what remains after the fee is deducted.

Example: estimating take-home proceeds

For an illustrative $100 payment with a 3.49% percentage fee plus a $0.49 fixed fee, the estimated processing fee is $3.98 and net proceeds are $96.02. These are example inputs rather than an assertion about your current PayPal account.

Example: asking the buyer to cover fees

To net $100 under the same example rate, the exact unrounded gross-up calculation is (100 + 0.49) ÷ (1 − 0.0349), then rounded upward to cents and verified against the rounded processing fee. The calculator handles this automatically.

Important factors that may change actual charges

PayPal fees can vary by product, account, transaction type, currency conversion, country, and other conditions. Refunds, disputes, chargebacks, micropayments, and special merchant agreements may follow different rules. This calculator does not estimate taxes or currency conversion spreads.

For the fee terms that apply to you, check PayPal's official U.S. merchant fee information.
