Understanding the result

Percentage fee

The variable portion of a payment-processing charge rises with the transaction amount. A 3.49% rate on $100 contributes $3.49 before the fixed fee.

Fixed fee

Some methods add a per-transaction charge. That makes small purchases more expensive as a percentage of the sale than large purchases.

International payments

For many US commercial transactions, PayPal publishes an additional 1.50 percentage points when receiving an international payment. Currency conversion may create separate costs.
