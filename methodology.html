How our calculations work

PPFees uses your entered percentage, fixed fee, and optional other fee. The values are estimates, not a live quote.

Payment-to-net formula

Fee = round(payment × percentage rate + fixed fee + other fee, 2 decimals). The net amount equals payment minus the rounded fee.

Net-to-request formula

To estimate a charge that will leave a desired net amount, the initial gross-up is (target net + fixed fee + other fee) ÷ (1 − percentage rate). The result is rounded upward to the nearest cent and checked against the rounded fee.

Multiple transactions

Fees are calculated and rounded for one transaction first. That fee and the resulting net are multiplied by the transaction count. This is not a model of batch discounts or tiered processing plans.

Limitations

Real-world charges can involve additional transaction categories, tax treatment, dispute fees, refunds, special arrangements and currency conversions. Confirm rates with the payment provider before invoicing or making financial decisions.
