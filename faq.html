Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator use live PayPal fees?

No. Rates are illustrative examples or amounts you enter. Official rates vary by product and transaction. Check your merchant account and PayPal's current published fee pages.

How do I calculate the amount to request?

Choose “How much should I request?”, enter your desired net amount, and set the percentage and fixed fees. The tool finds a gross amount that covers the estimated processing fee.

Can I calculate several payments?

Yes. Set the number of transactions. Each transaction is assumed to have the same payment amount and fee schedule; the tool rounds each fee before multiplying totals.

What about international payments and conversion fees?

Enter any known additional charge in the “Other fee” field. Exchange-rate spreads, cross-border rules and taxes are not independently modeled.

Is PPFees affiliated with PayPal?

No. PPFees is an independent educational calculator.
